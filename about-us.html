Our Journey Begins

PCSUnited was created from lived experience — years of service, leadership, family transitions, and the real pressure that comes with making major financial decisions during a move. The mission is simple: help Active Duty members, veterans, and military spouses understand their options before they commit.

Designed as a modern military FinTech platform, PCSUnited goes beyond basic calculators. It combines military pay context, base-level housing insight, financial readiness tools, and AI-supported guidance to help families make confident, informed decisions.

As a retired Air Force veteran with a master’s degree in Engineering Management from Johns Hopkins University and current graduate studies in Duke University’s MSQM: Business Analytics program, Josue Orozco built PCSUnited to give military families a clearer, smarter way to navigate PCS moves, housing decisions, and financial readiness.

Our Founder

As founder and CEO, Josue’s mission is to build a platform that earns trust through transparency. PCSUnited is designed around a clear methodology: use military-specific data, apply disciplined financial logic, and explain results in plain language. The platform reflects a simple belief — military families deserve tools that are smarter, clearer, and built by someone who has lived the same decisions.

Josue’s background combines military leadership, engineering management, psychology, and business analytics. He holds a bachelor’s degree in Psychology from Penn State, a master’s degree in Engineering Management from Johns Hopkins University, and is currently pursuing Duke University’s MSQM: Business Analytics program. That combination shaped PCSUnited’s foundation: human-centered design, structured decision-making, financial modeling, and clear communication for families who need answers fast.

Methodology page

PCSUnited was not created to overwhelm users with data. It was built to translate complex military pay, BAH, housing costs, affordability limits, and market conditions into practical guidance. The goal is to help military families understand whether a move makes financial sense, what risks they should watch, and what choices may protect their long-term stability.
