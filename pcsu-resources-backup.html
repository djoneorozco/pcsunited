Turn uncertainty into a confident PCS strategy.

PCSUnited was built to simplify one of the most stressful parts of military life: relocation decisions. Instead of jumping between disconnected calculators, spreadsheets, and housing websites, PCSUnited creates a connected decision-first experience that helps military families understand affordability, evaluate local markets, and confidently prepare for their next assignment.
PCSUnited uses TheWing.ai decision infrastructure to help military families understand affordability, housing pressure, and PCS readiness before making major financial decisions.
