Our Story

The Founders.
Builders at heart

Orvimo was co-founded by Elena Moritz, a workflow systems architect, and Raj Patel, a machine reasoning specialist. Their combined expertise laid the foundation for agentic automation that thinks and executes.
‍
Orvimo attracted support from top innovation believers including Maya Delgado, alongside strategic guidance from global startup accelerator organizations. Early development was also influenced by private investors who previously backed developer-centric automation ecosystems.

Orvimo was born from a simple belief: automation should feel natural, intelligent, and accessible to everyone. What began as a research experiment is now a builder-loved platform shaping how teams design workflows using AI agents.

Built through continuous innovation in our lab, Orvimo evolves with every workflow created. Our mission stays rooted in empowering builders, startups, and teams to automate smarter, faster, and greener. Founded by a small group of automation pioneers, Orvimo emerged to remove friction between humans, tools, and AI. The founders built early prototypes focusing on visual workflow design, multi-step AI agents, and predictive credit management. Today, the company operates with a core mission supported by continuous research inside Orvimo Labs.

Our Journey So Far

From an idea to empower growth through education and coaching, here’s how Pathwise has evolved—one milestone at a time
2017
The Idea Takes Shape
Inspired by the need for accessible coaching and structured learning, Pathwise was envisioned as a platform to guide individuals and teams toward personal and professional growth
2018
First Programs Launched
We introduced our Foundations Program and Leadership workshops, creating structured pathways for skill-building and confidence development.
2020
Expanding Our Reach
Pathwise grew beyond in-person sessions, offering online seminars and hybrid learning options to reach students, professionals, and teams worldwide.
2022
Community & Success Stories
With a growing alumni network, Pathwise highlighted client successes through case studies and testimonials, strengthening our community impact.
2024
Scaling with Innovation
We launched new advanced strategy tracks and integrated digital tools, making it easier than ever for clients and schools to access coaching, track progress, and achieve measurable outcomes.
