PCS Solutions for
The Military Community

Permanent Change of Duty Station (PCS) are stressful at any stage of your military career due to growing responsibilities. Home purchase becomes an option during this time, but often not properly vetted. PCSUnited was created to facilitate this process while providing comprehensive and unbiased anwers. We are not a Selling Platform, we are a Non-Profit Platform build to support our Military Community.

Built for Military Families

PCSUnited was created to help military families better understand housing decisions through structured guidance and financial clarity — not to pressure users into purchasing a home. Accounts are free, core tools remain accessible, and member information is never sold to third-party advertisers or lead-generation platforms.

Why PCS Decisions Matter

A PCS move can impact far more than just your next mortgage payment. Housing decisions may affect long-term financial flexibility, monthly cash flow, commute quality, savings potential, and future PCS readiness, making smarter planning critical for military families.
