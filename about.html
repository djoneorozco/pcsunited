PCS  With Financial Confidence

Military Families PCS Blindly, Making financial Decisions without Clear data.
Below are the 3 main biases and errors military members make during pcs.
#1. Overestimated Affordability
Many military families assume their BAH determines what they can afford.In reality, mortgage payments, insurance, taxes, and local market conditions often make homes significantly more expensive than expected.

‍
#2 Buying Wrong Home
Relocating under tight timelines leads many families to purchase homes that do not fit their long-term financial situation or lifestyle needs.Without structured financial analysis, buying decisions become rushed and reactive

‍
#3 Ignoring Market Conditions
Every PCS location has a different housing market.  Some Cities and surrounding Communities, have Strong Buyers Opportunities. While other may have r higher expensive home compared to your specific BAH and Base Pay.
