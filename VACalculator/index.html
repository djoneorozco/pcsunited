Retirement & VA Calculator FAQ

What does this calculator estimate?

It estimates projected military retirement pay, estimated VA disability compensation, and combined monthly income based on the selected retirement and disability inputs.

Does household status affect VA compensation?

Yes. VA compensation can change based on rating level and dependent status, which is why this tool includes a household-status selector.

Does this already solve CRDP and CRSC perfectly?

Not yet. This version is best treated as a strong planning estimate. Advanced concurrent receipt logic can be layered in during the runtime phase.
