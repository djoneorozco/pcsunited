Frequently Asked Questions

Why Do I need to Create an Account?

Account are necessary to provide Enhanced Data Analytics and A.I Concierge. Account enable greater analytics without sacrificing security measures.

Is my Information Secure?

We ensure all data is safeguarded, We request an e-mail passcode retrieval to ensure email authentication. Your data is never sold, or used by third parties to avoid security vulnerabilities.  

Are there any hidden fee associated with Account?

PCS United is not a for profit organization, therefore no cost are associated with our services. Accounts are required only for enhanced performance to avoid security vulnerabilities by storing data in local storage.

Does PCS United Sell or Use my data for profit?

No, PCS United will never sell or provide your data to any third party. Accounts information is Only required to enhance data analytics and avoid security vulnerabilities using local storage.

Can I delete my Account?

Yes, you can delete your account at any point for any reason. You will still be able to use our services but won't access enhanced analytics and A.I services.

What is TheWing.ai

TheWing.ai is our Software as a Service (S.A.A.S) platform. This premium FinTech service takes our platform to new heights, by going beyond basic calculators and static pages to an modern and intuitive dashboard. Finally providing you with the service you deserve.

Built for service members, veterans, and military families who need clearer PCS, housing, pay, and financial readiness decisions before the move gets expensive.
