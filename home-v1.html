PCSUnited is a veteran-owned platform designed to help military families make smarter financial decisions during a Permanent Change of Station.Unlike traditional military resources that provide general information, PCSUnited focuses on decision-based financial analysis.

Using this data, PCSUnited generates clear financial guidance to help families determine:

• Whether buying or renting is the better option
• What home price range is financially safe
• When to begin the home buying process
• What financial risks may exist at a given duty station

This allows service members to approach their PCS with confidence, clarity, and a structured plan.
